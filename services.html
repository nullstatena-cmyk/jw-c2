Services

What we clean, and how a job runs.

Everything here is reachable from the ground with a pole, at a home, an apartment community, or a storefront. If a job needs height, we will say so up front.

Window types

Sorted by what it takes to clean.

Glass is not all the same amount of work. Splitting it this way is how the quote stays honest instead of being a guess with padding built in.

Standard windows

Up to about 3 × 5 ft

Single-hung, double-hung, casement, and awning. The bread and butter of most jobs.

  • Exterior only, or inside and out
  • Frame edges wiped down
  • Ground and second floor from grade

Picture & large panes

Over about 3 × 5 ft

Fixed feature windows. More glass in one piece means more care getting a clean finish edge to edge.

  • Worked in sections to avoid drying marks
  • Priced per pane, not per square foot
  • Interior side needs furniture moved back a foot

Sliding glass doors

Per door panel

Patio and balcony doors. The track is included because it is always the part that actually looks dirty.

  • Both sides of each panel
  • Track vacuumed and wiped
  • Handle and frame cleaned down

French & grid panes

Divided lites

Small panes separated by bars. Slow work, and the type most often skipped by cheaper quotes.

  • Each pane cleaned individually
  • Muntins and bars wiped, not just glass
  • Quoted by pane count, so ask for a walkthrough

Screens & tracks

Add-on

The cheapest way to make a noticeable difference. Clean glass behind a dusty screen still looks dusty.

  • Screens removed, rinsed, dried, reseated
  • Tracks vacuumed out
  • Sills wiped

Storm windows

Add-on

A second exterior pane over the main window. Priced separately because of the removal and refitting.

  • Removed and cleaned both sides
  • Main window cleaned underneath
  • Refitted and checked before we leave
Scope limits

What we do not do.

Listed plainly so nobody wastes a visit finding out.

Above two stories

Our reach ends at roughly 25 feet from the ground. Anything above that needs a ladder, lift, or rope crew, and that is outside what we do.

Occupied rental interiors

At apartment communities we work vacant units and common areas. Entering an occupied resident unit needs a different consent process, so we leave it alone.

Pressure washing

Not our trade. Driveways, walkways, and siding need different equipment and a different quote.

How a job runs

From first call to paid.

Step one

Quote

Send a rough window count, the types, and how many floors. Most homes can be quoted without a visit. Larger or unusual places get a free walkthrough.

Step two

Schedule

Agree the scope and a date, in writing. Recurring customers pick a slot and keep it. Nothing gets added to the job later without asking you first.

Step three

Service

We turn up when we said, work the agreed scope, and walk it with you before leaving if you are home. Anything we find that needs flagging gets mentioned the same day.

Step four

Invoice

Invoiced on completion, payable within seven days. Card, transfer, or check. No deposit to book and no charge if you cancel with a day's notice.
