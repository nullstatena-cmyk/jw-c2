Before you ask

Three things worth knowing up front.

Payment

Invoiced on completion, payable within seven days. Card, transfer, or check. No deposit to book, and no charge for a quote or a walkthrough.

Scheduling

Usually within the week for homes. Cancel or move a booking with a day's notice and there is no charge either way.

Reach

Ground and second floor, roughly 25 feet up. If part of your place is out of reach we will scope around it and tell you, rather than quietly skip it.
